Viscous is a cat in a diving suit full of milk now. There are lööps.

The abilities

  • Splatter: a bowl of milk.
  • The Cube: a jelly mould.
  • Puddle Punch: a paw.
  • Goo Ball: the cat goes for a swim.
  • The gun: fires lööps.

What's in it

  • A new hero model, effects for every ability, new HUD art and new sounds.
  • Fair play: cosmetic only. His outline, hitboxes, warnings and cues stay Valve's.

Install

  • Close Deadlock.
  • Click Install with Deadlock Mod Manager at the top of this page, or copy files/0000-loops-viscous/pak20_dir.vpk into Deadlock/game/citadel/addons/ (the zip's README covers the gameinfo.gi line).

Uninstall by deleting the pak, or removing the mod in the mod manager. The builds it's proven on are listed at the top of this page; if the cat looks wrong after a patch, turn it off until a new version is out.
