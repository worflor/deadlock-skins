Lööps Viscous

the cat: "bröther, may i have some lööps."
the lööps: (float just out of reach)

A cat asked for some lööps. Nobody said no fast enough.

So there is now a fat long-haired calico in Viscous's diving suit. Her head is in the glass dome, right where his face was, looking up at three cereal rings that float just above her nose. She can see them. She cannot have them. She has made her peace with this. (She has not.)

The goo is whole milk. The bones are cereal. The diving kit is white kitchen enamel with a navy rim, chipped where a good bowl gets chipped. The gun is a cereal dispenser with a jar of rings on top, and it fires lööps.

Her body hangs in the torso where his stick figure used to be, her belly a beat behind every step. Her tail comes out through the milk at the back and swings when you jump and turn. Her ears, paws and whiskers move too. The rings stay where they are. She checked.

The abilities, made breakfast

  • Splatter. Knocked off the counter. The milk goes with its enamel bowl, and the bowl spins down to rest upside down in the spill, rings floating round it. You know exactly who did this.
  • The Cube. Turned out of a jelly mould. The mould pops off and leaves a wobbling block of set milk with cereal in it. Whoever is inside is as easy to see as in Valve's cube.
  • Puddle Punch. Making biscuits. Two little paws tread in the warning circle, then one big calico paw, toe beans and all, swats up out of the puddle. The warning stays Valve's green: the enemy still gets told.
  • Goo Ball. The good one. He rolls up into a glass ball a third full of milk, and the cat rides along inside in her bowl. She swims about the milk as the ball moves, turns to face where you're going, holds her tail up as a rudder and paddles harder the faster you roll. The milk tips back when you launch and sloshes when you hit a curb. When it bursts, one big ring rolls off on its own, curls in and rattles flat.
  • The gun. Rings in a gob of milk, a splash of milk at the muzzle, the jar of lööps riding on top.

What's in it

  • The whole hero: the cat, the milk, the cereal, the enamel kit and the dispenser, with physics on the tail, belly, paws, ears and whiskers.
  • Every ability's effects re-made as above.
  • New HUD art: hero cards, the portrait (in the top bar she is cross-eyed at a lööp on her nose), ability icons, and a name logo that reads VISCÖÜS.
  • A sound set built from physical models: milk sloshing in the suit when he moves, rings rattling, enamel clanks, the dispenser's detent and squirt. It is laid into Valve's own sounds, so every cue keeps Valve's timing, length and loudness. His voice is untouched.
  • Three re-keyed animation clips: the Puddle Punch and Goo Ball casts, and a hero-select pose.
  • Fair play: cosmetic only. His outline is Valve's own goo shell (the tail is the one bit outside it, and only just), the warnings keep Valve's shape, size, timing and colour, and no hitbox, stat or cue changes. The cat is not load-bearing.

Install

  1. Close Deadlock.
  2. Copy files/0000-loops-viscous/pak20_dir.vpk into your Deadlock/game/citadel/addons/ folder, or import the unpacked folder with Deadlock Mod Manager (Mods Library, Add mods, Open export file..., pick mod-interchange.json). By hand, gameinfo.gi needs the line Game citadel/addons above Game citadel; the zip's README has the steps.

Uninstall. Delete the pak you copied (or the mod in the manager).

Built for Deadlock build 6871 and proven in game on build 6868. A game update can break a mod: if the cat looks wrong after a patch, turn this off and wait for a new version.

What is not in it. No file of Valve's goes in unchanged (every file was compared against the game's own at the same path). The sounds and some effects are built on the game's own recordings and effects, reworked. The cat, the cereal and all the art are original, and the cereal is nobody's brand.
