Maid Lash: N°7, Grand Service

Dinner is served.

Mina: "You look ridiculous."
Lash: "You made it."

The House of Mina Ha. New York. Look No. 7.

It was meant as an insult.

(He would like it noted that it did not work.)

After two panned misunderstood seasons, the house needed a body for look No. 7, and Mina had a particular joke in mind for the man who keeps calling her little miss fashionista: a servant's livery. The inventory card still calls it Nuit Rouge No. 7, "The Help." He has asked us to change that. We have not, and will not.

Jacob Lash put it on to prove nothing could make The Lash look bad. He looked in the glass, said "oh, hell yes", and hasn't taken it off since.

It has his name across the back like a fight robe, because he insisted. His title belt sits at the waist, seven red stones, also because he insisted. The collar squares his shoulders the way a fighter carries them on the walk to the ring; the corset holds him the way hand wraps do. ("The ab support is NOT a corset.")

He embroidered the apron himself. Badly. With total confidence. ("Artfully," he says.)

The Lash is no one's servant. He simply serves. (That line was his. He'd like credit.)

The inventory

Every piece of his kit was reissued by the house and catalogued like it. He renamed most of them. These are the names that survived.

  • Service à Trois, No. 7. The gauntlet. Three barrels, three courses, served in order. (Dessert is also a punch.)
  • Spool N°7. The grapple, rewound in rouge ribbon. The old rope clashed with the apron.
  • Mademoiselle. The feather duster. She has a name, and he uses it mid-fight. Do NOT ask her to dust.
  • The Centrepiece. The lace doily his Ground Strike leaves on the street. Devastating to be under.
  • Service for One. The cloche. When the lid comes down, the table's been cleared.

What's in it

(The house wrote this part. He tried to add "and it's me" to every line.)

  • A complete outfit on Lash's own body: a parlourmaid's livery cut like a sailor uniform, a hooped bell skirt, a looped-up train, lace, and a bare band of thigh. ("Where it counts.")
  • Cloth that moves like a real crinoline. The skirt swings, the train bounces, the ribbons trail half a beat behind him, so everyone knows where he's just been.
  • Every ability re-dressed: ribbons, doilies, porcelain, rose petals and a cloche, each one drawn from a piece of the uniform.
  • New HUD art: hero cards and ability icons.
  • 86 re-keyed animation clips, including the curtsy. ("It's a bow.") It's a curtsy.
  • A sound set built from physical models of bells, porcelain, keys and satin. His voice is untouched. He would not have it any other way.
  • A hero-page showcase with its own backdrop. He's feeling his oats.
  • Fair play: purely cosmetic; hitboxes and ability cues unchanged. He doesn't need the help. (Pun his.)

Install

  1. Copy the pak into your Deadlock addons folder, or import the bundle with Deadlock Mod Manager.
  2. Optional: run install-scene.cmd to add the hero-page showcase (it builds from your own game files).
